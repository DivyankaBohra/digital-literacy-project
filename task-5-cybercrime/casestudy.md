# Cybercrime Case Study

**Type of Cybercrime:** UPI / Online Payment Fraud  

**Scenario:**  
A student from VIT Bhopal listed a second-hand gadget for sale on an online marketplace. Shortly after, they received a message from a person claiming to be a buyer. The buyer agreed to pay instantly and sent a QR code, saying it was required to “receive” the payment. Trusting the message, the student scanned the QR code and entered their UPI PIN. Within minutes, money was deducted from their bank account instead of being credited. The fraudster then attempted another transaction, but the student realized the scam and stopped further loss.

**How it Happened (Step-by-Step):**  
1. The student lists an item online for sale.  
2. A fraudster contacts them pretending to be a genuine buyer.  
3. The fraudster sends a QR code or payment request claiming it is for receiving money.  
4. The student scans the code or approves the request.  
5. The student enters their UPI PIN.  
6. Money gets deducted from the student’s account.  

**Who is Targeted:**  
Students, online sellers, and people unfamiliar with how UPI transactions work.  

**Consequences:**  
- Financial loss  
- Unauthorized bank transactions  
- Stress and panic  
- Reduced trust in digital payments  

**Lesson Learned:**  
Never scan a QR code or enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Always verify payment requests carefully before approving them.

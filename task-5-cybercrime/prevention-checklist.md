# Stay Safe Online Checklist for College Students.

- Never share your OTP, UPI PIN, or banking details with anyone.  
- Do not scan QR codes to receive money; scanning always sends money.  
- Always verify the identity of the person before accepting or approving any payment request.  
- Check URLs and app sources before entering personal or financial information.  
- Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on all important accounts.  
- Avoid using public Wi-Fi while making online payments or accessing banking apps.  
- Install apps only from trusted sources like official app stores.  
- Regularly monitor your bank transactions and report any suspicious activity immediately.  

## UPI / Financial Safety Tips
- Never approve “collect request” payments unless you trust the sender.  
- Double-check the receiver’s name before sending money through UPI.

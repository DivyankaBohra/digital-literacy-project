For this task, I used Canva to design my digital literacy infographic. Canva is easy to use and provides a variety of templates, icons, and design elements that helped me create a clean and visually appealing layout. My infographic explains the meaning of digital literacy and highlights its importance for students in today’s digital world. It also includes key points about safe internet practices, such as protecting personal information, avoiding suspicious links, and maintaining a positive online presence.

The design uses simple language, icons, and headings to make the information easy to understand. I focused on keeping the content clear and not overcrowding the page so that viewers can quickly grasp the message. One challenge I faced was selecting the most important points to include, as digital literacy is a broad topic. However, this helped me learn how to summarize information effectively. Overall, this task improved my understanding of digital safety and the importance of responsible online behavior.

In today’s digital world, poor communication can lead to serious misunderstandings. One common situation is when a student sends an informal or unclear email to a professor. For example, if a student writes a message without a proper subject line, greeting, or clear explanation, the professor may not understand the request or may ignore the email. This can result in missed deadlines or lost opportunities.

Such situations can be avoided by following proper email etiquette. The message should be clear, polite, and well-structured. Including a subject line, formal greeting, and complete information helps the receiver understand the purpose quickly. Maintaining professionalism in communication is important for academic and future career success.

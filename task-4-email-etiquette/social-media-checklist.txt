Social Media Do’s and Don’ts

Do’s:
1. Use strong and unique passwords for your accounts.
2. Think before posting anything online.
3. Keep your profiles private and secure.
4. Verify information before sharing it.
5. Respect others and maintain polite communication.

Don’ts:
1. Do not share personal information like phone number or address.
2. Do not click on suspicious links or unknown messages.
3. Do not post offensive or inappropriate content.
4. Do not spread fake news or rumors.
5. Do not use weak or common passwords.
